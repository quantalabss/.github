### About QuantaLabs

QuantaLabs is a deep-technology research and protocol engineering lab specializing in the transition to a post-quantum digital economy. We help Indian fintechs, institutions, and crypto companies complete **Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) audits** and migrate to NIST post-quantum standards ahead of India's DST FY 2027-28 deadline.

### Our Ecosystem

- **[QuantaCipher API](https://quantacipher.com)**: The world's first post-quantum encryption SaaS. Secure your enterprise data with NIST-standard Kyber-1024 in two lines of code. HIPAA-ready, zero-trust architecture.
- **[Quantachain](https://quantachain.org)**: A highly-scalable, quantum-resistant layer-1 blockchain built on the Falcon-512 signature scheme.
- **CBOM Audits & Migration**: Comprehensive infrastructure scanning, algorithmic mapping, and clear roadmaps for replacing legacy RSA/ECC with Kyber and Dilithium.

### Core Technologies

* **Kyber-1024 (FIPS 203)**: Post-Quantum Key Encapsulation Mechanism (KEM)
* **Dilithium/Falcon-512 (FIPS 204/205)**: Post-Quantum Digital Signature Algorithms
* **Zero-Trust Architecture (ZTA)**: Cryptographically enforced data sovereignty
* **Rust & WebAssembly**: High-performance cryptographic primitives
